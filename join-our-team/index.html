Who can apply

The team we are looking for

Students, fresh graduates and self-taught learners are all welcome. What we value most is character and effort.

  • Hardworking and consistent. You show up, finish tasks and improve with feedback.
  • Learning by doing. You learn practically, on real projects, not only from videos.
  • Growth before money. You care more about skills and long-term growth than quick income.
  • Honest and reliable. You communicate clearly, meet deadlines and work well in a team.
  • Basic setup. A laptop or phone and a reliable internet connection.

Internship questions

Is the internship paid?

Year one is a free learning year with no salary and no joining fee. After one year, salary starts for team members who complete the year and continue with the team. The amount and terms are shared in writing before year two.

Which roles are open?

Graphic Designer, Digital Network & Marketing, and AI Application Engineer. Choose one in the form, or choose "Not sure yet" and we will help you decide.

Do I need experience?

Not always. Tell us what you have tried, and share any projects or links. We may give a short task before confirming a place. Attitude matters more than a perfect CV.

Can I join from another city?

Tell us your city when you apply. We will confirm how the internship runs for your location.

How many hours a week?

The internship is part-time, so your studies and other commitments can continue. We agree weekly hours with you at the start.

What do I get at the end of year one?

A certificate of completion, a portfolio of real work, honest mentor feedback, and the start of a salaried role with the team.
