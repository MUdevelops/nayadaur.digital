Scope and questions

What is not included?
  • The ad budget, paid directly to Google; our fee is separate
  • Guaranteed leads, sales or return on spend
  • Google reviews ads and may reject some
How much budget do I need?

It depends on city, competition and goal. After a short review we suggest a starting budget, and say honestly if ads are not the right first step.

Who pays Google?

You do, through your own Google Ads account. We manage the campaigns.

Ads or a Maps profile first?

Usually a Google Business Profile first: it is free and builds trust. Ads add speed.

How soon will I see results?

Ads can show soon after approval, but useful results take a few weeks of data.
