Profile setup or cleanup

Name, category, address or service area, hours and contact details.

Who this is for

  • Shops, clinics, salons and workshops with a location

Scope and questions

What is not included?
  • Google decides verification and ranking; we cannot speed up or guarantee either
  • We never buy, fake or incentivise reviews
  • Business details must be real: no fake addresses or virtual locations
Is a Google Business Profile free?

Yes. Creating and managing a profile is free. You pay only for our setup work.

I have no shop. Can I still be on Google?

Often yes, as a service-area business that hides its address, if Google rules allow.

How long does verification take?

It depends on the method Google offers. We cannot control the timing.

Can I get more reviews?

Yes, from genuine customers. The QR system makes it easy for them to leave one.
