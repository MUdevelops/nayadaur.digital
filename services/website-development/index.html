Scope and questions

What is not included?
  • Domain registration, paid to the registrar (ideally in your name)
  • Hosting, as stated in your written quote
  • Text and photos, unless agreed in advance
  • Third-party fees such as payment gateways or couriers
  • A promised Google ranking: results depend on competition
How long does a website take?

Usually one to a few weeks, depending on pages and how fast you send content. A landing page is faster.

Do I need to buy a domain?

For an address like yourbusiness.com or .pk, yes. We help you choose and register it.

Can customers order through WhatsApp?

Yes. Product pages can open a WhatsApp chat with the item already named.

Can the site be in Urdu?

Yes. English, Urdu or bilingual layouts with proper Urdu typography.
