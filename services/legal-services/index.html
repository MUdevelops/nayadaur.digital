Questions about legal services

Who provides the legal services?

Shine Legal Associates, an independent law firm. NayaDaur Digital is a digital agency that introduces you and coordinates the design and digital side of your brand.

Is NayaDaur Digital a law firm?

No. We do not give legal advice. Legal advice, filings and representation come from Shine Legal Associates.

Do I need a logo before I register a trademark?

Not always. You can register a name first. If you want to register a logo, it helps to finish the design first. We can design it for you.

How much do legal services cost?

Fees depend on the service and the amount of work. Shine confirms the scope and fee in writing before anything starts.

Can you help if someone is already copying my brand?

Yes. Send us the links, photos and dates. Shine will assess your position and advise on notices, platform complaints and further steps. Outcomes cannot be guaranteed.
