Scope and questions

What is not included?
  • Guarantees of removal or outcome
  • Registration of brand names, which is a trademark matter
  • Court proceedings, quoted separately
Do I need to register copyright?

Protection generally arises when original work is created, but a registration gives you an official record that is useful as evidence. Shine confirms what applies to your work.

Does copyright protect a brand name?

Not usually. Names and logos used as brand identifiers are normally protected as trademarks. Shine can advise on both.

Who owns a logo or website I paid for?

It depends on the agreement. A written assignment makes ownership clear.

Can copied content be removed?

Often yes, through notices to the website, platform or seller. Results depend on the case.
