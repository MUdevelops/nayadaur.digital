  • Tax filing or full accounting software

What is an udhaar management system?

Software that records what each customer owes, their payments and balance, replacing the paper notebook.

Does it work in Urdu?

Yes. The interface and printed bills can be in Urdu, English or both.

Can you build something custom?

Yes. Tell us the repeated task and we will say honestly if a small tool is the right fix.
